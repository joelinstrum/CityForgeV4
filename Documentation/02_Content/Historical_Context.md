# Historical Context

## Default package and scope

The default setting is American history from roughly **1750 to the present**. It provides a recognizable calendar, architectural and transport changes, curated real-world headlines, and regional context. This is default content, not a hard-coded claim that CityForge only works in America. France, South Africa, or another setting should be able to supply different historical and cultural content through future packages.

History supplies setting and texture. Some events may affect trade, prices, or movement where that is well justified; many can remain headlines. A city builder cannot and need not simulate all of history. Regional differences should matter: American regions in 1750 were not interchangeable landscapes with different trees.

## Difficult history

The game may acknowledge slavery, Indigenous histories, war, and other human suffering in carefully curated context. **People must never become an inventory, production bonus, purchasable labor resource, or optimization target.** Avoid mechanics that imply Indigenous communities simply disappear as cities expand, or that reward a player for displacement. A culturally significant place can have a history that is not controlled by the player.

Historical omissions also require care: choosing not to simulate a subject is different from implying it never existed. Headlines, site descriptions, and region data should be accurate, specific, and reviewed. A source and editorial review process will be needed before factual historical content ships.

## Open questions

- What level of regional specificity can the default package support responsibly?
- Which historical events can change game conditions without turning suffering into gameplay currency?
- How will factual claims, dates, and culturally specific content be reviewed?
