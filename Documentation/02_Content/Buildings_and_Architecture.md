# Buildings and Architecture

## Buildings as usable places and historical artifacts

A building has a current function and a life story. At minimum, the design should allow construction or founding date, location, type or style, current use, and notable events to be shown where relevant. A building may be altered, reused, protected, or replaced. When demolished, its history may still belong in the city archive. The exact persistence model remains to be designed.

Architecture should reveal time and local conditions. Colonial, Georgian, Federal, Victorian, industrial, mid-century, and later forms were discussed as visual possibilities, but their exact availability requires research and regional variation. Older styles should not vanish when a new period begins. Economic conditions and material prices may influence what is built: imported brick can cost more without being forbidden.

The first V4 visual work should test how a building actually sits in the new terrain and lighting. Camera scale, shadows, roof and facade legibility, vegetation around lots, and seasonal appearance matter as much as an isolated asset render. Existing V3 buildings can be reviewed and selectively reused if they meet the target.

## Content questions

- How much of a building's history is authored, procedural, or generated from simulation events?
- Can players preserve a facade, adapt a use, or designate a district rather than only keep or demolish?
- How should architecture packages specify local styles without forcing every city into the same catalog?
