- **Recess single windows unless the user says otherwise.** Use a genuine wall opening and masonry returns, with sash/glazing behind the exterior face. The current townhouse target is 4 inches (0.1016 m). Keep stone lintels, sills and decorative masonry separate, and use slender, aged period sash rather than thick pristine frames. Validate an oblique close-up; a darker rectangle on a wall is not a recess.
