# Historic architectural refinement

Make City Forge architecture read as skilled period construction exposed to decades of normal weather. Keep every intervention subtle enough that viewers perceive authenticity without noticing an isolated effect.

## Preserve before refining

Freeze approved dimensions, proportions, opening locations, doorway composition, cornice and dentil rhythm, quoins, roof shape, and chimney placement unless the user explicitly requests a redesign.

## Depth and shadow

- **Recess single windows unless the user says otherwise.** Use a genuine wall opening and masonry returns, with sash/glazing behind the exterior face. The current townhouse target is 4 inches (0.1016 m). Keep stone lintels, sills and decorative masonry separate, and use slender, aged period sash rather than thick pristine frames. Validate an oblique close-up; a darker rectangle on a wall is not a recess.

- Prefer increased reveal depth, restrained projection, bevels, and real geometry over enlarged trim.
- Make windows read as openings in thick masonry, not assemblies attached to a flat wall.
- Emphasize entrances through deeper shadow and projection rather than additional ornament.
- Use historically appropriate masonry above openings: flat arch, jack arch, soldier course, gauged-brick lintel, or rubbed-brick arch.

## Controlled imperfection

- Vary brick tone, mortar value, projection, and erosion sparsely and without a visible pattern.
- Add occasional darker fired bricks and rare lighter replacements.
- Add slight roof-plane waviness, alignment irregularity, lifted corners, and small repair patches while preserving a well-maintained silhouette.
- Introduce tiny edge, paint, fascia, dentil, stone, and wood irregularities. Avoid obvious noise or identical repeated defects.
- Favor additional geometry when it materially improves sprite silhouette or shadow.

## Weathering limits

- Keep soot, dirt, discoloration, paint wear, and grade-level staining faint and localized.
- Maintain a restrained historic palette. Do not increase saturation to simulate material variation.
- Reject dramatic aging, abandonment, large damage, heavy grime, or stylized distress.

## Review

Inspect close perspective renders for repeated patterns, exaggerated effects, broken silhouettes, floating details, and changes that overpower the approved architecture. Compare before and after at matched camera and lighting.
