# Era content appearance

Government → Era Content selects the active district (or combined district members) appearance era. Automatic follows each original district's simulation year. An explicit appearance era does not change the calendar, building eligibility or unlocks. Save the region to retain it.

In the Lot Editor, **Era appearance…** provides a local appearance era, heritage preservation, paving by purpose, and per-asset Automatic/Keep/Hide exceptions. Apply the modal, then save the lot or apply the placed-lot landscaping and save the region. Cancel discards modal changes. Undo includes appearance settings. Building meshes are protected from prop visibility rules.

The selected path appearance dialog adds a paving purpose and a material lock. Automatic purpose uses Entrance for generated residential entry paths, Driveway for vehicle paths, and General for other paths. A locked material keeps its authored surface and is excluded from zoning material variation. Material IDs and geometry stay in saved definitions; the renderer resolves the current appearance without rewriting authoring data.

## Policy and content

`Resources/Config/EraAppearanceProfiles.json` supplies defaults for each timeline era. Art Deco (`modern`), postwar and contemporary defaults use concrete and filter transport by the existing vehicle catalog's availability. Earlier profiles preserve authored props and surfaces by default. Each profile may also hide explicit prop asset IDs. `soundSetId` is reserved content metadata; this pass does not implement audio-bank selection.

Precedence for props: protected building; local Hide; local Keep or heritage preservation; profile hidden list; vehicle-era availability. Unknown props remain authored. Exceptions apply to asset IDs within the lot, including repeated instances.

Precedence for paving: individual path lock or heritage preservation; custom material for the effective purpose; era profile default; authored material. A custom blank means keep the authored material. An unavailable private material leaves the authored surface visible; the editor reports missing concrete. District road shoulders use the profile's General material while preserving carriageway geometry and existing sidewalk eligibility.

District profiles apply to lot props, authored freight actors, ambient traffic, placed transport characters and streetscape transport. Lot overrides affect lot props, lot surfaces and traffic generated by that lot. Lot heritage exceptions do not change district road shoulders. The existing Art Deco Rolls-Royce is registered for ambient traffic. Clothing and general NPC demographic pools retain their existing behavior.

## Persistence and refresh

All fields are additive: `RegionDistrictInfo.appearanceEraId`, `LotDefinition.eraAppearance`, `LotCustomization.appearanceConfigured/appearance`, and path `eraRole/materialLocked`. Missing fields preserve historical defaults. Original district member IDs persist in combined districts. Region loading restores the appearance era after rebuilding district metadata and before entering the district. Unknown persisted era/material IDs and invalid exception lists are rejected.

An explicit profile change toggles existing registered prop instances and changes path materials/UVs in place. It refreshes ambient traffic and street shoulders without rebuilding buildings, grading terrain, changing path geometry, moving roads, or rewriting freight job state. Delayed characters remain hidden until population is ready. Appearance-only landscaping edits use this refresh; changed cosmetic geometry updates only the edited lot.

## Known content gaps

No compatible Art Deco motor freight model is supplied. Obsolete freight actors are inactive, with their saved jobs, cargo and positions retained; those deliveries pause until compatible content or an older appearance is selected. A passenger car is not a freight replacement. Later-era motor traffic is only shown where eligible private models exist. Era-specific audio banks and period-specific pedestrian clothing still need content and integration.

## Validation

`CityForge.Lots.EraAppearanceChecks.Run` checks backward defaults, prop exceptions, purpose paving, locks, protected buildings, additive customization, invalid data and a real disposable cfregion roundtrip. It writes only to the project Library (or `CITYFORGE_ERA_CHECK_OUTPUT`) and verifies that its read-only source save remains byte-identical. Full native Play QA additionally switches an existing district with loaded freight, checks original object IDs and mesh vertices, confirms concrete assignment, and saves/reloads the profile and cargo.
