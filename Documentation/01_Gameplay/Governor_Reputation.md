# Governor Reputation

## Scores with personality

The player oversees a region, so the proposed framing is **Governor** rather than Mayor. Numeric measures communicate precision; descriptive classifications communicate the kind of place and play style those numbers represent. Categories under discussion include Wealth or Prosperity, Culture, Health, Happiness or Quality of Life, Environment, Preservation or Legacy, and Connectivity. The final set and formulas are open.

Each category can have its own title ladder. **Illustrative examples only:** Wealth of $1.2 million might read *Industrial Conqueror*; Culture 24, *Functionalist*; Health 83, *Dr. Governor*; Happiness 50, *Equalizer*. A low Culture score may describe function over form, not failure. An environmentally poor industrial region might earn *Smokestack Governor*: a legible description of its path, not a moralized penalty label.

There is also an **overall reputation** reflecting sustained development and distinctive decisions. *Explorer* and *Founder* were discussed as possible early titles; after that, paths could branch toward commerce, civic life, regional planning, preservation, or other identities. The game observes the player's actions rather than asking the player to select a class. Distinctions or tags sit alongside the main title: *Rail Pioneer*, *River Trader*, *Historic Preservationist*, and the like. Combinations can make a region feel specific.

The score and titles should provide small goals without forcing a campaign. Show current standing, relevant contributors, and nearby opportunities in understandable language. Some distinctions might be discoverable. Reputation should respond to sustained conditions and major accomplishments rather than flip after one bad year. Humor and critical connotations can have a place if the wording remains descriptive.

All categories, thresholds, classifications, and distinctions should be moddable in principle. The exact data model and player-facing transparency are open.

## Open questions

- Which scores are absolute, relative to era, or relative to a region's scale?
- How are long-term reputation and short-term conditions blended?
- How much of the next title's criteria should be shown?
